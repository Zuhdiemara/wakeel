# Card disputes policy (Sahm Bank, fictional)

## 1. Scope
This policy covers disputes about mada debit card and credit card transactions: duplicate charges, charges for goods or services not received, wrong amounts, cancelled transactions that were still charged, and transactions the customer does not recognise.

## 2. Time limit to report
A customer must report a disputed transaction within 60 days of the transaction date. Disputes reported after 60 days are referred to a specialist and are not eligible for an immediate refund.

## 3. Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge is two or more transactions at the same merchant, for the same amount, within 24 hours, where the customer made only one purchase. The bank refunds the extra charge after checking that the transactions are identical in merchant and amount. The original transaction is not refunded.

### 3.1 Evidence for duplicate charges
No receipt is required when the ledger shows identical transactions at the same merchant within 24 hours. If the amounts differ, the case is treated as a wrong amount (section 5).

## 4. Goods or services not received
The customer must first contact the merchant and wait 15 days for a response. If the merchant does not resolve it, the bank opens a chargeback with the card scheme. A chargeback can take up to 45 days. A provisional credit is given only for amounts up to 1,000 SAR.

## 5. Wrong amount
If the charged amount is higher than agreed, the bank refunds the difference only, after the customer provides a receipt or invoice showing the agreed amount.

## 6. Unrecognised transactions and fraud
If the customer does not recognise a transaction, the card must be blocked immediately and a replacement issued. Fraud cases are handled by the fraud team, never by automated agents. The customer is not liable for transactions made after the card was reported lost or stolen.

## 7. Refund limits for automated handling
An agent may propose a refund of up to 5,000 SAR per case. Every refund requires approval by a bank employee before money moves. Refunds are credited to the original card or account within 3 working days of approval.

## 8. Cancelled transactions still charged
When a merchant confirms a cancellation but the charge remains, the bank refunds the full amount once the cancellation confirmation is provided.

## 9. Communication with the customer
The customer receives a case reference, the expected timeline, and the decision in Arabic or English, as they prefer. Decisions that refuse a refund must give the reason and the policy section.

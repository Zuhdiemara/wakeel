# Card fees and charges (Sahm Bank, fictional)

## 1. Annual fees
The classic credit card has no annual fee. The platinum credit card costs 500 SAR a year, waived if the customer spends 30,000 SAR in the year.

## 2. Foreign currency transactions
Transactions in a currency other than Saudi riyals carry a foreign transaction fee of 2.5% of the amount. This fee is shown as a separate line on the statement and is not a duplicate charge.

## 3. Cash withdrawals
Cash withdrawals with a credit card cost 3% of the amount, with a minimum of 75 SAR. Withdrawals with a mada debit card at Sahm Bank ATMs are free.

## 4. Late payment
A late payment fee of 100 SAR applies if the minimum payment is not received by the due date.

## 5. Card replacement
Replacing a lost or damaged card costs 25 SAR. Replacement after fraud is free.

## 6. Disputing a fee
A customer may dispute a fee within 60 days. Fees charged by mistake are refunded in full after review.

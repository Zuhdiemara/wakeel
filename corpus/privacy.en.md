# How we handle your data in disputes (Sahm Bank, fictional)

## 1. What we collect
For a dispute we use the transaction records on your account, the details you give us, and any receipts you upload. We do not ask for your full card number, PIN or password, and you should never send them.

## 2. Automated processing
An AI assistant helps prepare your case. It can read your transactions and propose a refund, but it cannot move money. A bank employee reviews every refund before it is made. You can ask for a human to handle your case at any time.

## 3. Retention
Dispute records are kept for 5 years, as required for financial records, and then deleted.

## 4. Your rights
Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, subject to legal retention duties. Contact us through the app or any branch.

## 5. Where your data is processed
Customer data is processed and stored in Saudi Arabia.
